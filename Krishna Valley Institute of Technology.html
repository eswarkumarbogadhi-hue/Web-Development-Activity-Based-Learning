Admissions procedure

Four steps from application to classroom.

  1. Register online

    Create an account with your email and phone number.

  2. Enter your scores

    Add your entrance rank (EAMCET or JEE) and Intermediate marks.

  3. Upload documents

    Marks memo, rank card, ID proof, and photo.

  4. Attend counselling

    Choose your branch and pay the first-year fee to confirm your seat.
